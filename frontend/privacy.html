Official Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective immediately for all EV Charge Hub users

1. Introduction

EV Charge Hub ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is a smart Electric Vehicle charging management and slot reservation service. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of our users. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website, web application, and related services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide smart EV charging discovery and slot booking services, we collect only the necessary information, including:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified email address, and strong encrypted password (stored using one-way bcrypt hashing).
  • Contact Verification: Optional mobile phone number used strictly for account security and two-factor OTP verification.
  • Electric Vehicle Profile: EV make, model, battery capacity (kWh), and compatible connector types (e.g., CCS2, Type 2, CHAdeMO) to recommend appropriate chargers.
  • Charging & Slot Bookings: Station IDs, reserved time intervals, session duration, and booking history.
  • Payment Records: Transaction IDs, wallet balances, and receipt references generated via our integrated payment gateway (Razorpay). We never store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, or CVVs on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for legitimate service purposes:

  • Authenticating your identity and preventing unauthorized access to your account.
  • Reserving and holding your charging bays at selected EV stations.
  • Sending automated charging status updates, booking receipts, and OTP verification codes.
  • Computing accurate charging duration and pricing estimates for your EV battery.
  • Maintaining security, preventing fraud, and resolving technical issues.

4. Data Storage & Security Standards

We implement modern industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data:

  • All network traffic is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/SSL).
  • User passwords are encrypted using salted bcrypt hashing algorithms with work factor 10.
  • API access is protected using JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with automatic expiration and rate limiting.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers.

5. Third-Party Integrations

To deliver map navigation and payment processing, we work with trusted service providers:

  • Mapping: OpenStreetMap and Leaflet for displaying station locations and driving routes.
  • Payment Gateways: Razorpay for processing digital transactions securely under PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your data. At any time, you may:

  • View, update, or edit your profile information from your user dashboard.
  • Add or remove saved electric vehicles from your vehicle garage.
  • Request full deletion of your account and associated booking history by contacting our support team.
